## Role Overview

Led front-end development and contributed to end-to-end feature implementation, most notably on the ESSAR Vehicle Tracking System — a real-time fleet tracking and reporting platform built with AngularJS on a PHP and MySQL backend.

## Key Responsibilities

- Led front-end development and end-to-end feature implementation across tracking and reporting modules

- Developed reporting modules with dynamic charts and graphs to turn vehicle data into actionable insights
- Collaborated with backend developers to integrate PHP/MySQL APIs with the AngularJS front end
- Participated in requirement analysis, technical discussions, and solution design

## Technical Skills Used
