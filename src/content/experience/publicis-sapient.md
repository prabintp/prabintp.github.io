Responsible for overseeing end-to-end technical strategy, driving AI adoption across client engagements, and ensuring that complex GenAI and Agentic AI systems are deployed securely and reliably in production environments.

- **Enterprise Architecture**: Designing scalable, maintainable software solutions aligned with enterprise architecture standards and business requirements

- **Team Leadership**: Leading cross-functional teams, mentoring engineers, and driving technical excellence across complex, multi-stakeholder programmes
