## Overview

Scalable, multi-tenant community and development authority platform built on a monorepo architecture, offering customised booking and investment options.

## Role: Front-End Lead

Drove technical delivery while collaborating closely with business stakeholders to gather requirements and translate business objectives into scalable technical solutions.

## Key Contributions

### Technology Evaluation & POCs
- Benchmarked technologies and evaluated the technical feasibility of third-party integrations
- Led POCs for cookie management, analytics implementation, and integration with **Adobe Experience Manager (AEM)**
- Explored **Universal Experience Editor (UEE)** capabilities and immersive digital experiences
- Piloted AI-assisted development using **Slingshot** to turn Figma designs into production-ready code and accelerate design analysis

### AWS Migration Planning
Led migration planning from the existing environment to a client-managed AWS environment:

- Dependency analysis and migration planning
- Phased execution strategy and risk identification
- Coordination of client-side prerequisites, laying the foundation for the migration and future implementation
