## Overview

Scalable multi-tenant travel and tourism platform for Diriyah Gate Company delivering lead generation, CMS-driven content, and analytics insights.

## Role: Lead Engineer

### Spec-Driven Development
Served as Lead Engineer for Spec-Driven Development — designing, writing, and maintaining the reusable components, execution structures, and framework assets that enable AI tools to reliably deliver software integrated with Figma designs, JIRA, and AI workflows. Owned and operated an autonomous development workflow using **Claude Code**, **Cursor**, and **Slingshot**, and worked with engineering, architecture, and business teams to turn requirements into delivery.

### Production Readiness
Contributed to application security, performance optimisation, caching strategies, and image and media delivery optimisation to get the platform ready for UAT and production.

## Technical Architecture

- **Next.js** with server-side rendering (SSR) and static site generation (SSG)
- **React** and **TypeScript** for type-safe component development
- **Tailwind CSS** and **Material-UI** for consistent, responsive design
- **GraphQL** for efficient data fetching and state management, with **AEM** as CMS
- Analytics and tracking with **Google Tag Manager** and **Google Analytics**
